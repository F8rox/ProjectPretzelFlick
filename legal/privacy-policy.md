# PretzelFlick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

PretzelFlick ("the app") is a Reddit Devvit app. A subreddit's moderators install it to post discussion threads for anime episodes. This policy explains what the app handles.

## What the app stores

For each subreddit that installs it, the app stores the following in Reddit's Devvit storage, scoped to that installation:

- The moderators' settings (automatic posting on or off, and a posting delay in minutes).
- The AniList numeric IDs of the shows the moderators chose to track.
- The IDs and links of the discussion threads the app created, and the scheduled job records for upcoming episodes.
- An activity history of up to 200 entries, each with a show ID, an episode number, a time, and a short reason (for example, "posted" or "air time moved").

## What the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about Reddit users or moderators. It does not read or store users' comments, messages, or profiles. To recover from an interrupted run, it reads the titles and authors of its own subreddit's most recent posts, and only to find threads the app itself created. It stores nothing from that read.

## Third-party services

To learn when episodes air, the app sends requests from Reddit's servers to AniList (`graphql.anilist.co`). A request contains only AniList show IDs and a time range. It contains no information about users, moderators, or subreddits. The app stores only the episode numbers, air times, titles, and official streaming links that AniList returns. AniList's own privacy policy applies to its service.

## Where data lives and for how long

All data stays in Reddit's Devvit storage. The app does not copy it to any other server or service. Reddit's platform controls how long installation data is kept and what happens to it when the app is uninstalled. The app's own activity history keeps only the newest 200 entries.

## Changes

If the app starts handling more data (for example, if it later adds per-user ratings), this policy will be updated first, and the date above will change.
